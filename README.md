# About Me

I am a developer fascinated by the inner workings of computation — from CPU instruction sets and assembly programming to high-level AI models and full-stack applications. My projects often explore the intersection of systems-level understanding and practical automation, building tools that solve problems for myself and my community.

I approach programming as both craft and art: understanding the constraints of memory, logic, and networking allows me to design software that is efficient, reliable, and innovative.

# What I Build

AI & Automation: Designing AI-driven applications, such as my portfolio receptionist, which gathers visitor project details and sends actionable information to me via Discord. Future plans include fully autonomous AI “crew members” for administrative, analytic, and support tasks.

Full-Stack Applications: Front-end with Angular/Next.js, back-end servers, CI/CD pipelines, and deployment on platforms like Vercel, with database orchestration via Docker & MariaDB.

Systems & Embedded Programming: Experimentation with C, C++, Assembly, Arduino breadboards, and low-level memory management.

ML & Simulation: Training AI agents with PyTorch to navigate 3D environments in Unity, as well as experimenting with LLM models and other AI reasoning tasks.
